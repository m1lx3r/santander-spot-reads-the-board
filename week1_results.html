What I changed

The starter takes the biggest 4-sided shape. Mine takes the whitest one.

Auto-Canny + CLAHEEdge thresholds follow the photo's median brightness, so dim rooms still show the frame.
Every candidateCollect all convex quads ≥ 3% of the image, nested ones too, instead of the first big one.
Whiteness scoreMedian brightness × (1 − saturation) inside each quad. A whiteboard is the whitest flat thing in a classroom.
Largest of the tiesWithin 6% of the best score, keep the biggest, so a box drawn on the board doesn't win.
